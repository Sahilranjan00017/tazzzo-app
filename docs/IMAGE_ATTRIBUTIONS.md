## Withdrawn 2026-09-08
`cat_cleaning.jpg`, `cat_oil.jpg`, `cat_skincare.jpg` showed third-party product
packaging (Jif, Oleina, Wardah) — competitors' trade dress, barred regardless of
licence. `cat_oil.jpg` and `cat_skincare.jpg` also carried unverified licences.
`cat_baby.jpg` was a photograph of an identifiable infant rather than a product.
Those four categories now render the tinted emoji tile until licensed,
brand-free photography exists.
